How to pitch it

  1. Write, don't phone — a letter or secure message leaves a record.
  2. Say the money is from a third party or one-off and won't be available for long.
  3. Start at 30%. Their counter is usually the real floor.
  4. Ask for the entry on your credit file to be marked satisfied (or partially satisfied at worst).
  5. Pay only after you have the acceptance in writing.
